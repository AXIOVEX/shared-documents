## Methods and validation (summary)

The full specification and validation record for this edition (spec 009, graduation-horizon release) is an internal Axiovex artifact, held in the workforce study's private repository. It is not published, so it is not linked here; the earlier relative link pointed at a location that does not exist in this repository and has been removed. The methods, in brief, from this edition's own public documents:

- Evidence was gathered from the public sources registered in the [source registry](source-registry.json), with retrieved values kept in [observations](observations.json) and projection figures in [projections](projections.json); the [evidence review](evidence-review.md) records how sources were corroborated and where they disagreed.
- Coverage: county data reach July 2026, state data reach August 2026, and national sentiment reaches final September 2026. Each figure is used at its own vintage, never silently as current.
- Graduation horizons are decision horizons for program review — short credentials for 2027, associate pathways for 2028, bachelor's programs for 2030, longer professional pathways for 2032 and beyond. They are not occupation expiry dates, and this edition declares no program obsolete on a speculative date.
- Every institution and employer pathway in this edition is a proposal. No participation by any named institution or employer is implied.
- The distinctions the narratives keep throughout: proposals are not results; workers are not jobs; AI exposure is not displacement; a credential is not competence; and a placement is not, by itself, proof that a program caused the job outcome.
